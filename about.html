About Me

I am an aspiring cybersecurity professioinal with strong interest in Security Operations (SOC), threat detection, and defensive security. I focus on developing practical cybersecurity skills through hands-in projects involving SIEM monitoring, attack simulation, incident analysis, and system hardening.

I enjoy working with security tools, analyzing logs, investigating suspicious activity, and developing Python-based automation tools to improve security workflows.
